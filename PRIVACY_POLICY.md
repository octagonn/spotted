# Spotted Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** July 25, 2026  
**Contact:** [privacy@spotted.app](mailto:privacy@spotted.app)

> This Policy explains how Spotted collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use the App and related services (the “Service”).

## 1. Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

- **Account information:** Email address, or—if you use Sign in with Apple—your Apple-issued identifier and the email and name fields you choose to share.
- **Profile information:** Username, display name, optional avatar, optional bio, home gym, and weekly schedule.
- **User Content:** Photos, captions, reactions, replies, and direct messages.
- **Friend graph:** Friends, removals, and requests sent or received.
- **Legal acceptance:** Accepted Terms and Privacy Policy versions, sign-in method, and acceptance time.
- **Communications:** Support messages and voluntary feedback.

### Information Collected Automatically

- Device, network, and app logs needed to operate and secure the Service, such as app and OS version, IP address, request time, and error details.
- Foreground location used only to verify check-ins at participating gyms. We do not collect background location.
- Expo Push Token, device platform, permission and category settings, delivery status, errors, and notification-open time when notifications are enabled.
- Limited local storage for sessions, preferences, and cached lists.

### Optional Product Analytics

Product analytics is off by default. If you opt in, we collect account-linked app opens, screen categories, selected feature actions, and device platform. Optional analytics does not include photos, messages, contacts, precise location, advertising identifiers, usernames, or free-form text. You can change the setting under **Settings → Privacy**.

### Information from Third Parties

- Apple provides identifiers and tokens needed for Sign in with Apple.
- OpenStreetMap contributors provide gym names, addresses, and coordinates for the gym directory. We do not send Personal Information to OpenStreetMap.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Provide, operate, maintain, and secure the Service.
- Verify gym check-ins and help prevent location spoofing.
- Deliver opted-in push notifications.
- Record acceptance of current legal terms.
- Suggest friends and gyms and personalize the feed.
- Enforce our Terms, prevent abuse, and protect users and the Service.
- Analyze usage with consent to improve features, performance, and reliability.
- Respond to support requests and communicate about the Service.
- Comply with legal obligations and enforce or defend legal rights.

## 3. Legal Bases for EEA and UK Users

We process information on the bases of contract performance, legitimate interests (including security, fraud prevention, and product improvement), consent (including push notifications, location, and optional features), and legal obligations.

## 4. How We Share Information

We do not sell Personal Information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not sell or share optional product analytics. We disclose information only:

- To service providers processing data for us, including Supabase (database, authentication, storage, and edge functions), Expo/EAS (builds, updates, and Expo Push Service), Apple (App Store, Sign in with Apple, and APNs), Google (FCM on Android), and our SMTP provider.
- To other users, limited to what you choose to share through profile, post, check-in, and visibility settings.
- When required by law or legal process, to enforce our Terms, or to protect rights, property, or safety.
- In a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, subject to standard confidentiality protections.
- With your consent in any other situation where we request and receive permission.

## 5. Photos and Camera

Photos taken through the App are uploaded to private storage controlled by your account and are accessed through short-lived signed URLs for authorized viewers. We access your camera roll only when you explicitly select a photo. When you tap **Save**, we request permission to add that specific photo to your library; saving does not grant access to other library photos.

## 6. Location

Location is requested only while you actively use the App. Coordinates verify proximity to a selected gym at check-in; we store the gym checked into, not your precise position, and do not share latitude or longitude with other users.

## 7. Notifications

If enabled, Supabase sends an Expo Push Token and notification content to Expo Push Service, which routes it through Apple APNs or Google FCM. Notifications may cover messages, friend requests and activity, post views and reactions, tags, communities, achievements, check-ins, streaks, and reminders. You can disable all push, individual categories, friend check-in scope, quiet hours, or device permission at any time. The in-app activity inbox may still show activity when push is disabled. Invalid tokens are removed, and signing out unregisters the current installation on a best-effort basis.

## 8. Retention

Daily posts and stories leave the current feed after 24 hours; archived posts remain until deleted. Direct messages remain according to messaging controls. In-app notifications and linked delivery rows are deleted after 7 days; notification deduplication logs after 90 days. Push tokens remain until sign-out, reassignment, invalidation, or account deletion. Optional raw analytics events are deleted after 90 days, and disabling analytics deletes retained raw events. Legal acceptance and account-level data remain with your account. Account deletion hides the account immediately and schedules permanent deletion after 30 days; signing in during that period restores it. Backups and security logs may persist for a limited period when needed for security, legal, or operational obligations.

## 9. Your Choices and Rights

- **Access and correction:** Review or update your profile in the App.
- **Deletion:** Delete individual posts or begin account deletion under **Settings → Danger Zone**.
- **Permissions:** Revoke camera, microphone, photo-library, location, notifications, and contacts permissions in device settings.
- **Product analytics:** Enable or withdraw optional analytics consent under **Settings → Privacy**.
- **Marketing:** We do not run marketing-email campaigns by default. Transactional sign-in and account-alert emails require sign-in functionality.
- **California:** You may have rights to know, delete, correct, limit sensitive-information use, opt out of sale or sharing, and avoid discrimination for exercising rights. We currently do not sell or share Personal Information.
- **EEA/UK:** You may have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port, or object to processing and to complain to your supervisory authority.

## 10. Children’s Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect Personal Information from anyone under 13. Contact us if you believe a child under 13 has provided information.

## 11. International Transfers

Spotted operates from the United States and processes information on US-based infrastructure. Information from outside the US may be transferred to and processed in countries with different data-protection laws.

## 12. Security

We use safeguards designed to protect information, including encryption in transit, encrypted credentials, row-level database security, signed URLs for private media, and team access controls. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 13. Changes

We may update this Policy. For material changes, we will notify you in the App or by email before they take effect. The effective date above identifies the current version.

## 14. Contact

Questions, privacy requests, or complaints: [privacy@spotted.app](mailto:privacy@spotted.app). We respond within 30 days, sooner where required by law.

This update is almost entirely your bug reports and ideas, shipped. Nearly every line below started as a screenshot someone sent us — keep them coming.

CAMERA AND POSTING
• Photo quality is dramatically better. The camera was quietly capturing photos at video resolution — it now uses the full sensor, and posts upload at much higher quality
• Picking a song now works the first time. No more selecting it twice, and it no longer disappears when you hit save
• Cleaned duplicate songs out of search and Top Songs Today — one Choosin' Texas is enough
• Dual camera posts now stay on your profile, and your grid shows the selfie as a corner picture-in-picture

WORKOUTS
• Routines are here. Save any workout as a named routine and run it whenever you want to hit it again — from My Routines when starting a workout, or straight from a past workout with Do This Workout Again
• Fixed mistyped weights sticking as your max. Records now recompute when you correct a set, so no more phantom 490 lb bench

STREAKS AND REST DAYS
• Streak notifications now match your real streak — rest days no longer secretly reset the count to zero behind the scenes
• Log a rest day and the locked page now says so, with a cancel button in case you change your mind and go anyway
• Rest days show in your streak calendar as moon days: they freeze your streak without inflating it, and they don't count toward your monthly total
• Deleting a post no longer makes the calendar look like it's lying. The gym day still counts, and opening it now says the post was deleted instead of "no posts"
• Everything above shows on your friends' calendars too, not just your own

TAGGED POSTS
• Swipe through all the posts you're tagged in as one feed, instead of opening them one at a time
• Add a post you're tagged in to your own story, with credit linking back to the original
• React to a tagged post straight from the notification

FRIENDS
• The suggested list now includes everyone. The old Discoverable setting was quietly hiding a big chunk of the app from suggestions and search — it's now called Contact matching and controls exactly that, nothing else
• A new friend's profile unlocks the moment they accept, no restart needed
• The pending requests list actually scrolls now
• The keyboard finally gets out of the way after you search for someone and add them

AT THE GYM
• Tightened gym detection. Driving past the front of your gym no longer counts as being at the gym

FIXES
• Photo zoom no longer gets stuck floating over the screen until you refresh
• Posts no longer randomly vanish from profile grids, and a post that failed to load shows an icon instead of a black tile
• Trophy descriptions no longer mention video verification that doesn't exist — your lifts, your word

Keep the feedback coming. Show up. Get Spotted.

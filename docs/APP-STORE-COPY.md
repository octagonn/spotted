## DESCRIPTION (max 4000)

The fitness social app built around actually showing up.

Spotted is a fitness community where your daily post comes from the workout itself — at the gym, training at home, or out on a run.

One post a day. Front and back camera. Gone in 24 hours.

No posting just to keep a feed alive. No perfectly curated fitness persona. Just you, your friends, and the days you actually put the work in.

SHOW UP. GET SPOTTED.
Your daily Spotted is tied to a real workout. Start a session, head out for a run, or train at home, and capture the moment while you are actually in it. Miss the day and there is nothing to post — that is the whole point.

BUILD YOUR TRAINING HISTORY
Every session adds up. Log lifts and cardio set by set, watch personal records land the moment you hit them, and keep a streak that only moves when you do. Your profile holds the whole story: streak, gym days, records, trophies, and your weekly split.

CHASE TROPHIES
Hit PRs. Build streaks. Train at 5AM. Lift at midnight. Show up on holidays. Trophies range from everyday consistency to milestones only a small percentage of members ever earn.

SEE WHO IS TRAINING
Find the gyms around you, see who is there right now, and know when your friends are mid-session. Training is easier when you can see the people doing it with you.

JOIN YOUR GYM'S COMMUNITY
Every gym has its own feed and chat. Post to the people you actually train around, not to strangers on the internet.

REACT WITH YOUR FACE
Instead of another like button, Spotted sends a real reaction. Your front camera captures your response and it goes straight to your friend.

SHARE THE REST OF IT
• Stories with text, drawing, colors, and music
• Video posts for PRs, heavy sets, and form checks
• Photo posts with up to four shots and friend tagging
• Direct messages, group chats, and voice notes
• A public feed for discovering lifters beyond your circle

BUILT TO BE PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
Your account starts private. You choose who sees your posts, your gyms, your streak, and your friends list. Gym location is never public. Everything you share can be exported or deleted whenever you want.

Your workouts already happen. Spotted makes them social.

Show up. Get Spotted.

## WHAT'S NEW IN THIS VERSION (max 4000)

This one is a big cleanup pass driven almost entirely by beta feedback. Thank you to everyone who sent notes — nearly every item below came from a tester.

GROUP CHATS AND MESSAGING
• Anyone in a group can rename it, the way it should have worked from the start
• Tapping a chat name now opens a full details page instead of a cramped popup: members, add people, mute, report, and leave
• Every chat now has a Media section showing all the photos and videos sent in it — tap any one to open it full screen
• One-to-one chats get the same details page, with the person's profile, mute, block, and report
• Removed the three-dot menu — everything lives behind the chat name now
• Rebuilt the menu that appears when you hold a message
• You can search your friends when starting a new message

CAMERA AND POSTING
• Drafts are rebuilt. There is now a Save as draft button on the post details page, a gallery of all your saved drafts with previews, and multi-select to delete them. Posting a draft removes it.
• Fixed posts being saved as drafts on their own, and the draft prompt appearing when you had not taken a photo
• The selfie camera no longer flashes the screen when flash is off
• Camera controls are consistent everywhere, with a self-timer and dual camera
• Home Gym is now called Custom Location, so it fits training anywhere
• Stories now unlock after you post your daily Spotted

MUSIC
• Explicit songs are now available — they were silently filtered out before
• Explicit tracks are marked with an E badge
• Suggested songs refresh every day instead of showing the same list

WORKOUTS
• Drag and drop to reorder exercises mid-workout, with haptics as they snap
• Finishing a workout now has a proper celebration, and it builds when you set PRs
• Records tab for your personal bests
• Fixed the muscle group filters getting cut off when picking exercises

PROFILE AND PRIVACY
• Edit your home gym without redoing onboarding
• New setting to hide your friends list from everyone, including friends
• Private profiles now properly hide friends lists
• Sharing your profile now actually includes a link
• Tightened up the profile layout
• Quiet hours are off by default so you stop missing evening notifications
• Post view notifications removed
• Restricted accounts are now genuinely silent

ACCOUNT
• Changing your password now uses the verification code we email you, instead of pointing at a link that never arrived
• You can connect Apple sign-in to an existing account, including with Hide My Email
• Download my data now works — you get a zip of everything on your account by email

FRIENDS
• Pull down to refresh on the Friends and Find tabs
• Add people directly from someone's friends list instead of only being able to message them
• New messages and friend requests now show up without leaving and reopening the app
• Fixed taunts failing and flipping back

Keep the feedback coming. Show up. Get Spotted.
